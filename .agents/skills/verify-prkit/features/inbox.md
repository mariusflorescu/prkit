# Inbox

The Inbox lists open pull requests across the machine's GitHub accounts in three groups: Needs your review, Yours, and Involved. Search, account and repository filters narrow the list and a sort orders it; the filters row stays pinned when the list scrolls. Each row's right column has two lines: a size chip (XS to XL) with a reading time, and a clock that always has its verb (`waiting 3 days`, `opened 5 days ago`, `updated 2 hours ago`). The fixture `<fixture>#1`, "Validate links and count clicks", appears once in Yours through `github.com/<owner>`. An account's error stays beside the other accounts' rows.

## Sub-features

- `inbox-list` lists open PRs from every ready account, one row per PR.
- `inbox-reasons` shows why each PR needs the user: `review-requested`, `authored`, `involved`.
- `inbox-account` shows the account each PR was found through. With a single account, rows show no account and the Account filter is hidden; the repository filter stays.
- `inbox-dedupe` lists a PR found by two accounts once, under the account with the stronger reason.
- `inbox-account-error` shows one account's failure next to the other accounts' rows.
- `inbox-states` has a loading skeleton, an empty state, and an error state with a retry.
- `inbox-filters` searches titles, repositories, authors and numbers, and selects one account. A filter hides the groups it empties; unfiltered, every group shows with its count.
- `inbox-triage` shows each row's size chip, reading time and clock. The chip is the larger of the bucket the changed lines fit and the bucket the files fit. The row's link carries one `title`, on every row: the size's numbers and reading estimate (20 lines a minute, half a minute per file), then the clock's evidence. A review request shows `waiting <duration>` (a `+` when the wait may be longer); a draft or a wait prkit can't state shows `updated ...` with the reason in that title. The wait comes from a second GitHub request, `PrkitReviewWaits`, sent right after the inbox request and stored with it, so its failures stay its own and the rows and their waits come from one moment.
- `inbox-sort` orders each group by Recently updated (the default), Waiting longest, Smallest first or Riskiest first, with the same tiebreak (last update, then key) and a stack kept together at its best-ranked layer. A sort reads nothing from GitHub. Riskiest first puts high before medium before low and a PR with no rated guide after every rated one, and inside each of those the Waiting longest order (the row shows that clock too). It reads each level from the review feed, so it reorders when a guide lands. Drafts and unknown waits sort after known ones; under Waiting longest the Yours and Involved rows go by when they were opened and say so.
- `inbox-risk` marks a row with the guide's quiet risk chip (word and three-dot meter, `[data-risk-tag]`) right after its `Guide ready` mark, once the PR's latest guide has a level. A PR with no guide, and a guide from before risk, show none. The level is the review feed's `risk`, so a new revision changes the chip with no reload. The chip stays at every width and holds the title line's height, so the row doesn't grow when it appears and a long title gives way to it. See [Guide viewer](./guide-viewer.md) for the chip's levels.
- `inbox-saved-filters` keeps the search, account, repository and sort with the server's workspace, so a reload and a relaunch show them again. Typing is saved once it pauses (400 ms), other changes (a sort included) at once, Clear filters leaves the sort alone, and filters that didn't change are not saved again. They come back with the first rows a load draws, from prkit's own answers (the workspace and the inbox) without waiting for GitHub, so a relaunch never draws a frame without them. An account the inbox no longer has, and a repository filter none of whose accounts are left, are let go on that load. While nothing shows and an account in view has nothing kept yet, the skeleton stays until it is read. A saved repository filter drops out once a whole repository list read after the save and the inbox no longer have the repository.
- `inbox-repository` filters to one repository from a searchable list of every repository the accounts can see (kept a day, "Refresh list" reads it again), grouped by owner with each one's inbox count. A repository where nothing needs you offers "Show open pull requests in owner/repo", read through the first account that sees it.
- `inbox-header-collapse` scrolls the title row away and pins the filters row, which takes a Refresh icon; the PR view's collapsing header, shared.
- `inbox-refresh` reads every account again past the ten-minute cache. Window focus does not refetch; the server marks answers stale when GitHub changes.
- `inbox-truncation` reports the number shown and the search's total.
- `inbox-guide` marks rows `Guide ready` or `Writing guide` from the app-wide review feed, and offers `Generate guide` on hover or focus for a PR without a guide, in place of the row's triage column (size, reading time and clock), which has a width that the action never exceeds. See [Guide generation](./guide-generation.md).
- `inbox-guide-feed` says so above the rows when prkit can't read the review feed (`alert "Couldn't read your guides"`, with prkit's words and Retry). If nothing was read, marks and risk chips are hidden and Riskiest first orders by wait; after a read, the last ones show, say they may be out of date, and keep their order. No row offers `Generate guide`, and every row stays. prkit tries again by itself after 2 seconds, then less often, up to a minute apart.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open `openUrl`; the Inbox is the first page.
- Return to the Inbox through the entry point in [Shell](./shell.md).

## Driving it with e2e

The journey in `tests/accounts-inbox.e2e.ts` checks the fixture's account and group, the second account's filter (when there is one), no matches, filter recovery, and account-bound navigation. `tests/inbox-triage.e2e.ts` checks the rows' size, reading time and wait with their evidence (light, dark, 900 px and 600 px), each sort's order with no GitHub request, a new review request, the saved sort after a reload and a relaunch, drafts, a request older than the 20 events GitHub shows, a waits read that fails or gives nothing for one PR, and filters saved before the sort, all on the fake GitHub; `accounts-inbox.e2e.ts` also checks the real accounts' rows read-only. `tests/inbox-triage.e2e.ts` also drives stand-in agent guides at several levels on the fake GitHub: the chips, the Riskiest first order and its saved choice, no GitHub read per row, a relaunch with a guide from before risk, a second guide at another level, and the CLI's order (see Risk chip below). `tests/inbox-filters.e2e.ts` checks the repository filter, the row action, the groups a filter empties, a PR merged while GitHub's search still finds it and the saved search and repository filter against the fake GitHub, the collapsing header against the real inbox and again on the fake's one account, and the saved account filter on an instance of its own with the real accounts. `tests/review-feed.e2e.ts` damages a guide record so the feed fails, on the fake with stand-in guides (see Review feed below). `tests/inbox-relaunch.e2e.ts` relaunches on the fake with two accounts while GitHub's answers to their verification are held, and records everything the list draws from the page's first script (see Relaunch below).

## Gotchas

- The repository list is read only once the filter opens, or on a load with a saved repository filter, at most 10 pages of 100 per account; an account cut short says "the first 1,000 of N repositories". Until it is read whole after the filters were last saved, a saved repository filter stays: an older list may predate the repository.
- Filters and the sort persist in the shared e2e instance. `openInbox` clears them through the UI; a journey that opens the Inbox another way fills the search itself.
- The inbox answers from cache for ten minutes, then from cache at once while it reads GitHub again in the background. A PR opened on GitHub a moment ago needs Refresh.
- Real accounts can have many PRs. Assert on the fixture row by name, not on a row count.
- A row shown under the wrong account is the worst bug this app can have. Always assert the account, not only the title.
- `Showing 100 of N` means the search was truncated, not that the other PRs are absent from GitHub. A sort orders only the PRs prkit holds.
- Dismiss the guide toasts (`button "Dismiss notification"`) before a screenshot; they cover the lower rows.
- The `[data-risk-tag]` styles live in the guide's CSS, which ships in the one app bundle. The chip has no styles of its own.
- A stack's layers sit together where its best-ranked layer is, so a sort's order is not monotonic across a stack. Assert on rows that aren't stacked.
- The clock's wording changes with the minute. Match `waiting (30|31) minutes`, not one number.
